References

The following academic literature, documentation, and textbooks provide further reading on Django view architecture and web request processing.

Documentation & Textbooks

  • Django Software Foundation (2024). Writing Views and Handling HTTP Requests.
    Django Official Documentation. Available at: https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/stable/topics/http/views/
  • Django Software Foundation (2024). Class-Based Views Overview and Generic Display Views.
    Django Official Documentation. Available at: https://docs.djangoproject.com/en/stable/topics/class-based-views/
  • Greenfeld, D. R., & Greenfeld, A. R. (2020). Two Scoops of Django 3.x: Best Practices for the Django Web Framework.
    Two Scoops Press. Chapters on FBV vs. CBV design guidelines.
  • Mele, A. (2022). Django 4 By Example. Packt Publishing.
    Comprehensive patterns on URL routing, MVT structure, and view inheritance.
  • Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India. Virtual Labs Project Guidelines & Academic Standards.
    National Mission on Education through ICT (NMEICT). Available at: https://www.vlabs.ac.in/
